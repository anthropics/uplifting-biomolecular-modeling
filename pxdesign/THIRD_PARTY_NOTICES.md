# Third-party notices — kit `pxdesign`

The kit's own files (everything outside `stock/`) are add-on code, tests and documents. `stock/` holds byte-identical copies of
two upstream projects, PXDesign and Protenix, at the pinned commits (STOCK.md §Pin, `stock/PINS.json`), each with its own `LICENSE`
file, and is never modified (PXDesign's copy leaves out two font files the kit does not use: STOCK.md §Pin); the third upstream project,
PXDesignBench, is not distributed in this tree — it is fetched from its
repository at install, at the pinned commit, under its own licence. No model weights and no compiled binaries are among the kit's own
files, and the kit binds no prebuilt kernel; the container recipes (`environment/Dockerfile`, `environment/apptainer.def`) copy the
shared library `common/opt_core` into the image beside the kit, and that library carries prebuilt GPU kernels (last row). The table
lists every third-party component that the kit's own files adapt, restate or wrap, and the licence texts that accompany the shared
