Stability model (stability.eqx)
-------------------------------
Trained on the "Mega-scale" protein folding stability dataset (licensed CC BY
4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The dataset authors are
credited below.

Dataset citation: Tsuboyama et al., "Mega-scale experimental analysis of protein
folding stability in biology and design", Nature 620, 434-444 (2023).
doi:10.1038/s41586-023-06328-6 / dataset doi:10.5281/zenodo.7992926

Data split from EsmTherm (https://github.com/SimonKitSangChu/EsmTherm),
MIT License.
