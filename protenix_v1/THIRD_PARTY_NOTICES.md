Data files. `opt/forward/v05_addon/inputs/p995_1brs.json` holds the amino-acid sequences of PDB entry 1BRS (barnase–barstar; wwPDB archive
data, CC0 1.0) and `inputs/msa/*/{pairing,non_pairing}.a3m` are single-sequence (query-only) alignment files made from those sequences —
no third-party database sequences are included. `opt/forward/v05_addon/lib/protenix_fpf_ditfast/CELLS.json` and `vectors.json` are
kit-generated validation records (device / stack words and sha256 digests of test outputs; no weights, no structure data), as is
`lib/fastln_prebuilt/manifest.json`. Model weights and data caches are not part of this tree or of the images built from it; `STOCK.md`
'Pin' says where `run.sh install --weights DIR` fetches them from and how they are digest-checked. Other run-time dependencies (Triton,
cuEquivariance, DeepSpeed, …) are installed from their own distributions under their own licences and are not redistributed here.
